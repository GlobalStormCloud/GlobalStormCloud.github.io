## What we collect, and why

### Just for visiting

Until you accept analytics, this site sets no cookies and loads no third-party
scripts. There is no advertising network and no advertising cookie.

### Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how this website is used: which pages are
visited, how visitors arrive and what devices and browsers they use. We use the
results to improve the site. Analytics runs only if you accept it in the cookie
banner. If you decline, or have not chosen yet, no analytics script is loaded and
no analytics cookie is set. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control
signal, we treat it as a decline. Analytics does not run on the documentation at
/docs.

- **What is collected:** the pages you view, your approximate location,
  information about your device and browser, and a random identifier kept in a
  cookie. Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses; Google uses the
  IP address of your request to provide and secure the service and to estimate
  your approximate location. We do not turn on Google signals or advertising
  features, and we do not send the contents of the demo form to analytics.
- **Cookies:** two first-party cookies on globalstorm.cloud: `_ga` (tells
  visitors apart) and `_ga_M2N1SW21M1` (keeps the session state). By default
  both last up to two years.
- **Legal basis:** your consent, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR; and, for storing
  information on your device and reading it, your consent under Article 399 of
  the Polish Electronic Communications Law (Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej).
- **Provider:** Google Ireland Limited provides Google Analytics to us and acts
  as our processor. Google may process the data in the United States and other
  countries; for those transfers Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy
  Framework and, in certain circumstances, on standard contractual clauses (see
  “Who else processes your data” below).
- **Retention:** Google Analytics deletes user-level and event-level data
  automatically once the retention period set in our analytics account has
  passed; for the standard version of Google Analytics, Google allows at most 14
  months.
- **Giving consent:** voluntary. Declining does not limit your use of the site.
- **Withdrawing consent:** at any time, with “Cookie settings” in the footer of
  every page, which reopens your choice. Withdrawing stops analytics and deletes
  the analytics cookies we can reach on this site; you can also delete cookies
  in your browser settings. Withdrawing does not affect processing carried out
  before. Your other rights are described under [Your rights](#your-rights).

### The demo request form

If you submit the form, we receive the fields you completed: your name, work
email address, company, what you said you were interested in, and your message.

The submission is sent through a Google Apps Script endpoint and stored in a
Google Sheet in our Google Workspace account, which only we can access. Google
acts as our processor and may process the data in the United States. We use the
submission to reply to you and to prepare a quote.

- **Legal basis:** Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in responding to
  business enquiries; and Article 6(1)(b) where you contact us on your own behalf,
  for example as a sole trader — steps taken at your request prior to a contract.
- **Retention:** kept while we are in contact and for up to 24 months after our
  last exchange, then deleted. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we will.
- **Providing your details:** voluntary, but we need them to answer your
  enquiry; without them we cannot reply.

### Email

If you write to us, for example at contact@globalstorm.cloud, we receive your
email address and anything you include in your message, and we use it to reply.

- **Legal basis:** Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in answering
  business correspondence; and Article 6(1)(b) where your message concerns a
  contract or a quote.
- **Retention:** the same as for form enquiries — up to 24 months after our last
  exchange, unless the correspondence must be kept longer as part of a contract
  or for legal reasons.
- **Mail provider:** our email is hosted by Google Workspace. Google acts as our
  processor and may process the data in the United States.

### Server logs

This site, including the documentation at /docs, is hosted on GitHub Pages
(GitHub, Inc., United States). When you visit, GitHub processes your IP address
and standard request data (such as the page requested, time, browser and
referring page) in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not
receive or store those logs; GitHub keeps them under its own retention rules.

- **Legal basis:** Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in operating a
  secure, working website.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us to:

- confirm what data we hold about you, and give you a copy (Article 15);
- correct anything inaccurate (Article 16);
- delete it (Article 17);
- restrict how we use it (Article 18);
- provide it in a portable format (Article 20);
- stop processing based on legitimate interest (Article 21).

You can withdraw a consent you have given at any time (Article 7(3) GDPR), for
example with “Cookie settings” in the footer; this does not affect processing
carried out before you withdrew it.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing, and we
do not profile you (Article 22 GDPR).

Write to [contact@globalstorm.cloud](mailto:contact@globalstorm.cloud) and we
will respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the Polish supervisory authority:
**Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych**, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa.

---
title: 7. Signing in to the dashboard
description: Open the customer dashboard, sign in with your issued credentials, and find the four main areas — Devices, Applications, Builds, and Deployments.
---

1. Open the customer dashboard URL provided by Global Storm in your browser.
2. Sign in with the email and password issued to your account.
3. You land on the dashboard with four main areas:
   - Devices — your fleet, health, and per-device detail.
   - Applications — your connected Git repositories.
   - Builds — CI/CD build history and security scans.
   - Deployments — the history of what was rolled out, and where.
